🚀 JobSwipe (or Your Project Name)
Swipe Right on Your Next Career Move.

An AI-powered job search platform that pairs a fun, friction-free "Tinder-style" swiping interface with dynamic, real-time resume tailoring.

🌟 Key Features
📱 Swipe to Apply / Save

Swipe Right: Instantly submit an optimized application or save to your "Interested" board.

Swipe Left: Pass on roles that don't fit your current career goals.

Super Like / Star: Highlight top-priority roles for immediate follow-up.

🎯 AI Resume Tailoring Engine

Parses job descriptions on the fly when you view a card.

Re-orders skill sets, highlights matching keywords, and rewrites bullet points to maximize ATS match scores.

Generates a custom, targeted PDF/Text resume instantly upon applying.

📊 Match & Application Dashboard

Track all your "Swiped Right" jobs in real time.

Monitor application statuses (Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer).

Direct messaging/notifications when recruiters express mutual interest.

🔍 Preference Filters

Fine-tune your "deck" by salary range, work environment (Remote, Hybrid, On-site), tech stack, and location.
